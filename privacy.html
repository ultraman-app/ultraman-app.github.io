Privacy Policy: Ultraman and its Claude plugin

Back to Ultraman

What we collect

Nothing. Ultraman has no accounts, no analytics and no telemetry. The plugin sends nothing to us.

What stays on your computer

Your project files (pipelines, requests, environments, run and load history) live in your own folder or git repository. Secret values live in an encrypted vault in your user folder (~/.ultraman), protected by a key only you hold. The MCP server talks only to the Ultraman app on the same computer.

What goes over the network

Only (1) the requests your own project defines, to the APIs you choose, after you approve them; and (2) at most once a day, an update check that reads the latest release from GitHub's public API (api.github.com). GitHub's own privacy policy applies to that request.

To turn the update check off, start Ultraman (and its CLI) with the environment variable ULTRAMAN_NO_UPDATE_CHECK=1; then nothing is requested.

What Claude receives

The results of the calls you approve, with secret values replaced by [secret:name]. What Claude does with them is governed by Anthropic's terms for your account.

Sharing and sale

We share and sell no data; we hold none.

Retention

Everything stays on your computer until you delete it; we keep nothing.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/ultraman-app/ultraman-app.github.io/issues. Last updated: November 2026.
